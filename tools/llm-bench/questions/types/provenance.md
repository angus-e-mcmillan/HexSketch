Each question is a step from [Chemistry by Design](https://github.com/chemistry-by-design/cbyd)
(Williams et al., arXiv:2611.24996, CC-BY 4.0): the route file it was taken from, the step
number in that route, and the paper the route was curated from. The answer to a reaction
question is the product the authors drew. Use this to check an answer against the literature.

The three `route-` questions and the five `retrosynthesis-` questions show a target and ask for
building blocks, so the step named above is the coupling the answer accepts, not a reaction the
model is shown. The `structure-` and `analysis-` questions show one molecule from that step and
ask about it; no reaction is drawn.
