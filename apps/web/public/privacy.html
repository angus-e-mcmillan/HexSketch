Privacy

HexSketch · last updated 25 October 2026

HexSketch has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. This page says what reaches our servers, and for how long.

hexsketch.com, the editor

  • The editor runs in your browser. What you draw stays on your computer: the page is not allowed to send it anywhere (its security policy blocks connections to other servers).
  • Your settings, saved styles and recent structures are kept in your browser's local storage, never sent to us.
  • A HexSketch link carries the structure after the #, which browsers do not send to the server.
  • Send Feedback (File menu) is the one thing that reaches us from the editor: your message, the email address if you give one, and your browser's name and version. We keep it to read and answer, and delete it on request. Your IP address is used only to limit how many messages can be sent a minute, and is not stored.

The HexSketch connector for AI assistants

When you add HexSketch to an AI assistant such as Claude as a connector (https://mcp.hexsketch.com/mcp), the assistant sends structures to our server to be drawn, named or checked, and the in-chat editor sends what you draw in it.

  • Structures sent to draw, name, analyze, convert or check are processed and answered, not stored.
  • An editor opened in a chat is kept on the server while you use it, so that you and the assistant work on the same drawing: the drawing, what the assistant showed in it, and a random id for the conversation. It is deleted automatically a day after it was last used. It is not linked to who you are.
  • We do not log what is drawn. Our host, Cloudflare, processes requests on our behalf and keeps its standard operational records (such as IP addresses and times) under its privacy policy.
  • What the assistant does with your conversation is covered by that assistant's own privacy policy.

If your structures must not leave your computer, run HexSketch locally instead: the hexsketch command and its local MCP server (hexsketch mcp) work offline and send nothing anywhere.
