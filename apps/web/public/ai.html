Chemists think in sketches. LLMs think in text. HexSketch lets chemists talk to LLMs with structures.

HexSketch puts a structure editor inside your chat with Claude. You draw, Claude sees the drawing. Claude answers by drawing back: structures, reaction schemes with reagents and conditions, highlights and notes.

Add it to Claude

Takes a minute. Nothing to install, no account.

  1. In Claude, open Settings › Connectors › Add custom connector.
  2. Name: HexSketch · URL: https://mcp.hexsketch.com/mcp · click Add.
  3. Start a new chat and type: Open HexSketch with ibuprofen.

Works on claude.ai and in the Claude desktop app. On a Team or Enterprise plan, an owner adds the connector first.

Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http hexsketch https://mcp.hexsketch.com/mcp

Keep everything on your computer: download hexsketch.mcpb, double-click it and click Install in Claude Desktop. It works offline. Use either this or the connector, not both.

Things to ask

  • Draw the synthesis of paracetamol from 4-aminophenol.
  • What is this? (after drawing something)
  • Highlight the stereocentres in this structure.
  • Suggest a protecting group for the amine and show the scheme.

Any other assistant

  • Draw in HexSketch, then Edit › Copy for AI and paste into the chat: it gives the name, SMILES, formula and masses, and a link back to your drawing.
  • Ask the assistant for a link like https://hexsketch.com/#smiles=CCO to open its structure here, ready to edit.

More
